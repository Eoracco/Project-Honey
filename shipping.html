Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this to track your package through the carrier's website.

Delivery Issues

If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, please check your tracking information first. If the issue persists, contact us at support@wildroot.us and we will resolve it promptly.

WildRoot Honey is not responsible for delays caused by the carrier, weather events, or incorrect shipping addresses provided at checkout.

Lost or Stolen Packages

If your tracking shows "delivered" but you have not received your package, please check with neighbors and your local post office. If the package cannot be located, contact us within 7 days of the marked delivery date and we will investigate and work toward a resolution.
