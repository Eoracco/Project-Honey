Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

We stand behind every product we sell. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we want to make it right.

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your order for any reason, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

Eligibility

  • Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Both opened and unopened products are eligible under our satisfaction guarantee.
  • One refund per customer per product line.
  • Refunds are not available for orders with confirmed delivery issues caused by incorrect address provided by the customer.

How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, please email us at support@wildroothoney.com with:

  • Your full name and order number
  • The email address used at checkout
  • A brief reason for your request (optional but appreciated)

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days.

Processing Time

Once your refund is approved, it will be credited to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Damaged or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged or items are missing, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the package. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no cost to you.

Contact Us

For any questions about returns or refunds, reach us at support@wildroothoney.com.
